How this screen works

Lanes. A — stock replacement: deep-ITM calls on the two nearest Januaries ≥365 DTE (0.85/0.70Δ). B — directional swing: first monthlies ≥30/≥60 DTE, 0.55/0.40Δ. C — debit verticals on B's chains, ~0.55Δ long / ~0.25Δ short (actual leg deltas reported, not the targets). D — calendars/diagonals, deliberately thin: no breakeven, BE/EM, P(BE) or carry, because a calendar's P/L at the front expiry depends on the back month's IV at that future date, a model this stack does not have. E — straddle + strangle, four gates, refusing most names is the lane working. F — defined-risk credit spreads.

E[R] is expectancyMean: mean P/L across historical N-session windows ÷ capital risked, held to expiry — no early close, no IV path — so it understates any structure that would realistically be closed early. A relative ranking, not a P&L forecast. ↑ marks unbounded upside scored only as far as the largest observed move.

cov vs P(BE). P(BE) is risk-neutral, off the chain's own IV (N(d2), never 1−|Δ|). Coverage is the measured frequency the stock actually moved past the breakeven, 1y and 3y reported separately and never averaged — their disagreement is the regime warning. gap = cov − P(BE) is not a pure vol signal: coverage contains drift and P(BE) is driftless, so on a trending name the drift term dominates — which is why gap renders with drift1y/3y directly beside it. A modest negative gap is expected: it is the variance risk premium, not a defect.

Lane F inverts direction. Its cov and P() are probabilities of the WIN — a credit spread wins by the stock not reaching the breakeven — the opposite reading from every long lane's "beyond breakeven". Same arithmetic, opposite meaning; the lane's column copy says "win".

Term structure inverts too. front − back positive is backwardation, and here that is hostile — front-dated premium is exactly what a buyer pays. (A premium seller reads the same sign as favourable.)

Vol state is tri-state. Cheap (rank ≤ 40 or IV/HV ≤ 0.95×) · not cheap · no basis yet — the third renders neutral, never dim: rank needs 60 days of collected IV samples and its absence is not a failure.

Liquidity floors: spread ≤ 15% of mid near-dated / 30% LEAPS, OI ≥ 10. A breach is flagged and dimmed, never dropped — untradeable must look untradeable, not missing.

Refusals are findings. A lane that declines to score names its own numbers (Lane A's coverage refusal at 365-session horizons is structural — the 3y history supports ~1 independent window against a floor of 4). The concentration warning renders only as its full label naming its window ("half the expected value from ONE 3y episode"), never a bare glyph, and nothing dims or reorders on it. Junk-IV guard: strikes quoting IV 4× outside their own expiry's ATM are excluded from selection and counted on the lane.

The directional tag (aligned/counter/out-of-horizon) is informational only — the rating measured a negative edge and reorders nothing here. "no tag" (lane doesn't take one) and "no read" (the rating store held nothing) are different states.
